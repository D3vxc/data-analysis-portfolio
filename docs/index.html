About

I came to analysis the long way round. Two years as a developer, cleaning and validating the data behind live products, taught me that most bad analysis starts upstream of the analyst. So I check the data before I trust it, and I write down what I could not verify.

My MSc dissertation asked whether a hybrid intrusion detection system catches attacks nobody has labelled. It does not, except in one narrow case. Publishing that instead of a flattering number is the part of the work I would defend first.
